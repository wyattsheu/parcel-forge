## User learning
Concept: a simulator integrates in discrete steps, so it does not reproduce the
textbook answer exactly. At dt = 1/240 s the cube "falls" about 2 mm further by
t = 0.1 s than the analytic 0.04905 m. That gap is the integrator, not a bug,
and it is why the tolerance is 3 mm rather than zero.
Experiment: change one parameter — `simulation.dt` in
`profiles/s1_smoke_v1.json` from 0.0041666… (1/240) to 0.0083333… (1/120) — and
re-run `./scripts/pf smoke`. Predict first: the overshoot formula 0.5*g*dt*t says
the error roughly doubles to about 4 mm, which now exceeds the 3 mm tolerance, so
`S1.free_fall_matches_analytic` should fail. That failure is correct behaviour.
Do not widen the tolerance to hide it; restore dt afterwards.
